Signs You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Palm Harbor

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.

Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.

What a Professional Brings to Emergency Water Damage Restoration

The visible water is the easy part of an emergency. What takes skill is figuring out where everything else has soaked in.
