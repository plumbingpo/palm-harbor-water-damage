A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Palm Harbor Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Palm Harbor and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

How to Tell You Need Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.
