Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Palm Harbor Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Palm Harbor, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

How We Handle Sewage Cleanup

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle sewage cleanup from start to finish:

1

Hazard Check and Containment

Our team assesses the extent of sewage contamination, identifies safety hazards and establishes containment to prevent cross-contamination of unaffected areas.

2

Extracting Sewage and Removing Materials

All standing sewage is extracted. Porous materials that were contaminated, such as drywall, insulation, carpet and padding, come out and go to proper disposal as biohazard waste.

3

Cleaning and Disinfecting

EPA-registered antimicrobials are used to disinfect what is left after a thorough cleaning of each surface. Meanwhile, air scrubbers fitted with HEPA filters pull pathogens and other contaminants from the air.

4

Dry-Out and Deodorizing

We dry out any moisture that remains using commercial equipment. An industrial deodorizing treatment gets rid of the sewage smell. Clearance testing confirms the space is safe for occupancy.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.
